English

Overview

Matchingo is a vocabulary match-3 game. You can play as a guest without an account. Optional sign-in (Apple, Google, or email/password via Firebase Authentication) lets you sync learning progress across devices using Cloud Firestore. We do not sell your personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Information we process

DataWherePurpose
Account identifiers (email, Apple/Google account ID, display name/photo if provided by the provider) Firebase Authentication Sign-in and account recovery
Learning progress (word memory stages, review times, exposures) On device; also Cloud Firestore when signed in Spaced repetition and “Learned” stats; cross-device sync
Preferences (language, music, SFX, legal notice acknowledgement) On device only Remember settings
Authentication technical data (Google Sign-In may process phone number, coarse location, device ID, usage/interaction and other account data; Firebase Auth may process diagnostic data) Processed by Apple, Google, and Firebase as applicable Complete sign-in, prevent abuse, maintain reliability, and provider authentication analytics

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In to provide optional accounts and cloud sync. These providers may process account and technical information on our behalf only to provide their contracted services. Google Sign-In’s SDK privacy manifest declares authentication-related coarse location, device ID, usage and other data for app functionality and provider analytics; Firebase Auth declares non-linked diagnostic data for analytics. We require service providers to protect personal information consistently with this policy and applicable law. Firebase processing is subject to Google Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What Matchingo does not directly collect

  • Matchingo does not request precise Location Services, Contacts, or Photos library access. Google Sign-In may process coarse location and related technical data as described above.
  • Microphone recordings are not uploaded to Matchingo or the developer’s servers
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
  • Advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking

Speech and audio

The app plays bundled pronunciation audio and may use on-device text-to-speech. Say & Blast only starts on devices that support Apple’s on-device English speech recognition. Microphone audio is processed on the device and is not uploaded to Matchingo or the developer’s servers.

Account deletion and data deletion

While signed in, open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud learning data for this app, and clears related progress stored on that device. Guest (local-only) progress is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data. Signed-in cloud data is retained while the account remains active. You can stop future sync by signing out, or remove the account and cloud data with Delete account. A failed cloud deletion stops the account-deletion process so it can be retried safely.

Children’s privacy

Matchingo is a general-audience app and is not listed in the App Store Kids Category. Guest play does not require an account. Users under 13 (or the applicable minimum digital-consent age in their region, if higher) may use guest mode but must not create an account. A minor above that threshold may create an account only with parent or legal-guardian authorization where required by local law. If we learn that personal information was collected from a child below the applicable threshold, contact us and we will delete it.

简体中文

概述

「Matchingo」是一款词汇消消乐学习应用。您可以不登录、以访客身份游玩。 可选登录（Apple、Google 或邮箱密码，由 Firebase Authentication 提供）后，学习进度可通过 Cloud Firestore 跨设备同步。 我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不使用第三方广告追踪。

我们处理的信息

数据存储位置用途
账号标识（邮箱、Apple/Google 账号标识，以及提供方返回的昵称/头像等） Firebase Authentication 登录与账号管理
学习进度（复习阶段、下次复习时间、曝光次数等） 本机；登录后同步至 Cloud Firestore 间隔复习、「已学」统计与跨设备同步
偏好设置（语言、音乐、音效、法律提示确认状态） 仅本机 记住设置
认证技术数据（Google 登录可能处理手机号、粗略位置、设备 ID、使用/交互及其他账号数据；Firebase Auth 可能处理诊断数据） 由 Apple、Google 与 Firebase 按实际功能处理 完成登录、防止滥用、维持可靠性及服务商的认证分析

第三方服务

我们使用 Google Firebase（Authentication 与 Cloud Firestore）、通过 Apple 登录及 Google 登录提供可选账号与云同步。 这些服务商仅为提供受托服务而代表我们处理账号及技术信息。Google Sign-In SDK 的隐私清单声明会为 App 功能及其认证分析处理粗略位置、设备 ID、使用及其他数据；Firebase Auth 声明会为分析处理不与身份关联的诊断数据。我们要求服务商按照本政策与适用法律提供同等保护。 相关处理适用 Firebase 隐私说明 与 Google 隐私政策。

Matchingo 不会直接收集的信息

  • Matchingo 不会请求精确定位、通讯录或照片图库权限；Google 登录可能按上述说明处理粗略位置及相关技术数据
  • 麦克风录音不会上传至 Matchingo 或开发者服务器
  • 不用于跨应用追踪的广告标识符
  • 不接入广告 SDK，也不进行跨应用追踪

语音与音频

本应用播放内置发音，并可能使用设备系统朗读（TTS）。「开口出击」仅在支持 Apple 设备端英语语音识别的设备上启动；麦克风音频在设备端处理，不会上传至 Matchingo 或开发者服务器。

注销账号与数据删除

登录后，请到 我的 → 删除账号。将永久删除您的 Firebase 登录账号及本应用对应的云端学习数据，并清除该设备上相关进度。 访客（仅本地）进度可在卸载应用或清除应用数据后删除。 登录后的云端数据会保留至账号删除。退出登录可停止后续同步；选择「删除账号」可删除账号与云端数据。若云端删除失败，账号删除流程会中止，以便安全重试。

Matchingo 是普通全年龄应用，不申请 App Store 儿童类别；游客游玩无需账号。 13 岁以下用户（如所在地区规定更高的最低数字同意年龄，则以该年龄为准）可以使用游客模式，但不得创建账号。达到该年龄但仍属未成年人的用户，仅可在当地法律要求的父母或法定监护人授权下创建账号。如果我们得知误收集了低于适用年龄门槛的儿童个人信息，请联系我们，我们将予以删除。
