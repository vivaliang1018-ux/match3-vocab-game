English

Overview

物品消消乐 is a vocabulary match-3 game. You can play as a guest without an account. Optional sign-in (Apple, Google, or email/password via Firebase Authentication) lets you sync learning progress across devices using Cloud Firestore. We do not sell your personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Information we process

DataWherePurpose
Account identifiers (email, Apple/Google account ID, display name/photo if provided by the provider) Firebase Authentication Sign-in and account recovery
Learning progress (word memory stages, review times, exposures) On device; also Cloud Firestore when signed in Spaced repetition and “Learned” stats; cross-device sync
Preferences (language, music, SFX, onboarding flag) On device only Remember settings

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide optional accounts and cloud sync. Firebase processing is subject to Google Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings for our servers
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
  • Third-party analytics or crash SDKs beyond what Apple/Google platforms may collect for App Store / Play distribution

Speech and audio

The app plays bundled pronunciation audio and may use on-device text-to-speech. Speech is processed on-device; we do not upload microphone audio.

Account deletion and data deletion

While signed in, open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud learning data for this app, and clears related progress stored on that device. Guest (local-only) progress is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us and we will delete it.

简体中文

概述

「物品消消乐」是一款词汇消消乐学习应用。您可以不登录、以访客身份游玩。 可选登录（Apple、Google 或邮箱密码，由 Firebase Authentication 提供）后，学习进度可通过 Cloud Firestore 跨设备同步。 我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不使用第三方广告追踪。

我们处理的信息

数据存储位置用途
账号标识（邮箱、Apple/Google 账号标识，以及提供方返回的昵称/头像等） Firebase Authentication 登录与账号管理
学习进度（复习阶段、下次复习时间、曝光次数等） 本机；登录后同步至 Cloud Firestore 间隔复习、「已学」统计与跨设备同步
偏好设置（语言、音乐、音效、引导标记） 仅本机 记住设置

第三方服务

我们使用 Google Firebase（Authentication 与 Cloud Firestore）提供可选账号与云同步。 相关处理适用 Firebase 隐私说明 与 Google 隐私政策。

我们不收集的信息

  • 不会为向我们的服务器上传而收集地理位置、通讯录、照片或麦克风录音
  • 不用于跨应用追踪的广告标识符
  • 除 App Store / 平台可能自带的统计外，不接入第三方广告分析 SDK

语音与音频

本应用播放内置发音；必要时使用设备系统朗读（TTS）。朗读在设备本地完成，我们不会上传麦克风音频。

注销账号与数据删除

登录后，请到 我的 → 删除账号。将永久删除您的 Firebase 登录账号及本应用对应的云端学习数据，并清除该设备上相关进度。 访客（仅本地）进度可在卸载应用或清除应用数据后删除。

儿童隐私

本应用不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。请勿为 13 岁以下儿童创建账号。 如认为我们误收集了相关数据，请联系我们删除。
