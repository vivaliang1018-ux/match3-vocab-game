## English

### Overview

Matchingo is a vocabulary match-3 game. You can play as a guest without an account. Optional sign-in (Apple, Google, or email/password via Firebase Authentication) lets you sync learning progress across devices using Cloud Firestore. We do not sell your personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

### Information we process

| Data | Where | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| Account identifiers (email, Apple/Google account ID, display name/photo if provided) | Firebase Authentication | Sign-in and account management |
| Learning progress (word memory stages, review times, exposures) | On device; also Cloud Firestore when signed in | Spaced repetition and “Learned” stats; cross-device sync |
| Preferences (language, music, SFX, legal consent) | On device only | Remember settings |

### Service providers

We use **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). See [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Information we do not collect

- Location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings for our servers
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
- Third-party analytics or crash SDKs beyond what Apple/Google platforms may collect for store distribution

### Speech and audio

The app plays bundled pronunciation audio and may use on-device text-to-speech. Speech is processed on-device; we do not upload microphone audio.

### Account deletion and data deletion

While signed in, open **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud learning data for this app, and clears related progress stored on that device. Guest (local-only) progress is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

### Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 简体中文

### 概述

「Matchingo」是一款词汇消消乐学习应用。您可以不登录、以访客身份游玩。可选登录（Apple、Google 或邮箱密码，由 Firebase Authentication 提供）后，学习进度可通过 Cloud Firestore 跨设备同步。我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不使用第三方广告追踪。

### 我们处理的信息

| 数据 | 存储位置 | 用途 |
|------|----------|------|
| 账号标识（邮箱、Apple/Google 账号标识，以及提供方返回的昵称/头像等） | Firebase Authentication | 登录与账号管理 |
| 学习进度（复习阶段、下次复习时间、曝光次数等） | 本机；登录后同步至 Cloud Firestore | 间隔复习、「已学」统计与跨设备同步 |
| 偏好设置（语言、音乐、音效、法律同意标记） | 仅本机 | 记住设置 |

### 第三方服务

我们使用 **Google Firebase**（Authentication 与 Cloud Firestore）。相关处理适用 [Firebase 隐私说明](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) 与 [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

### 注销账号与数据删除

登录后，请到 **我的 → 删除账号**。将永久删除您的 Firebase 登录账号及本应用对应的云端学习数据，并清除该设备上相关进度。访客（仅本地）进度可在卸载应用或清除应用数据后删除。
