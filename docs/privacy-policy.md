## English

### Overview

物品消消乐 ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first vocabulary game. We designed the App so that **you can play without creating an account**. We **do not sell your personal information** and **do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers** in the current version.

### Information we collect

**We do not collect personal information on our servers while you play as a guest.** If you choose to **sign in**, we store your learning progress in Firebase (Authentication + Firestore) so it can sync across your devices. We do not sell your personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

The App may store the following **locally on your device only** (via browser-style storage such as `localStorage`):

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Learning progress (words seen, mastery stage, review schedule) | Spaced repetition and「已学」stats; synced to your account in the cloud when signed in |
| Game preferences (language, music on/off, sound effects on/off) | Remember your settings |
| Onboarding completion flag | Show tutorial only once |
| Guest profile label | Display「访客玩家」in the Profile tab |

This data **never leaves your device** unless you back up your device through Apple iCloud or migrate to a new phone using a system backup that includes app data.

### Information we do not collect

- Name, email, phone number, or account credentials (no account system in v1)
- Precise location
- Contacts, photos, or microphone recordings
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
- Analytics or crash reports sent to third-party services *(update this section if you add Sentry/Firebase later)*

### Speech and audio

- **Pronunciation:** The App plays bundled audio files and may use your device's **built-in text-to-speech** when a recording is unavailable. Speech is processed on-device by iOS; we do not receive audio from your microphone.
- **Music & sound effects:** Audio files are bundled in the App. No streaming account is required.

### Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience vocabulary game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate user accounts or server-side profiles, we do not store children's personal data on our servers. If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information, contact us at the email above.

### Data retention and deletion

- Progress remains on your device until you **delete the App**, **clear app data**, or **restore your device** without including app data.
- Uninstalling the App removes locally stored progress from that device.
- We cannot restore deleted local data because we do not host copies.

### Your choices

- Turn off music or sound effects in Profile settings.
- Change interface language in Profile settings.
- Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.

### Third-party services

The current version does not integrate third-party SDKs for login, ads, or analytics. The App is distributed through the **Apple App Store**, which is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If optional image packs or external links are shown (e.g. third-party icon credits), tapping those links opens content outside the App under that provider's policies.

### International users

The App stores data locally on your device. No data is transferred to our servers in the current version.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when we add features (for example, account sign-in or cloud sync). We will update the "Last updated" date and, where required, notify you in the App or App Store listing.

### Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy:

**Email:** [support@yourdomain.com]

## 简体中文

### 概述

「物品消消乐」（以下简称「本应用」）是一款以本地运行为主的词汇学习游戏。当前版本 **无需注册或登录**，**不会在服务器上收集您的个人资料**，也 **不使用第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK**。

### 我们收集的信息

**我们不会在访客模式下在服务器上收集个人信息。** 若您选择 **登录**，学习进度会保存到 Firebase（Authentication + Firestore），以便跨设备同步。我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不使用第三方广告追踪。

本应用仅在 **您的设备本地** 存储以下信息（通过 `localStorage` 等本地存储机制）：

| 数据 | 用途 |
|------|------|
| 学习进度（已见词汇、掌握阶段、复习计划） | 间隔复习与「已学」统计 |
| 游戏偏好（语言、音乐开关、音效开关） | 记住您的设置 |
| 引导完成标记 | 首次安装后只显示一次教程 |
| 访客身份展示 | 在「我的」页面显示访客玩家 |

上述数据 **默认不会上传至我们的服务器**。若您通过 **iCloud 备份** 或 **整机迁移** 转移设备，是否包含本应用数据取决于 Apple 系统备份策略。

### 我们不收集的信息

- 姓名、邮箱、手机号或账号凭证（v1 无账号功能）
- 精确地理位置
- 通讯录、照片或麦克风录音
- 用于跨应用追踪的广告标识符
- 发送至第三方分析/崩溃平台的数据 *(若后续接入 Sentry 等，请更新本节)*

### 语音与音频

- **发音：** 本应用播放内置录音；若无对应录音，将使用设备 **系统朗读（TTS）**。朗读在设备本地由 iOS 处理，我们不会采集您的麦克风音频。
- **音乐与音效：** 音频资源打包在应用内，无需联网流媒体账号。

### 儿童隐私

本应用面向普通用户的学习场景。我们不会在服务器端主动收集儿童个人信息。由于当前无账号体系，我们不在服务器保存儿童用户档案。如您认为我们意外获取了个人信息，请通过上方邮箱联系我们。

### 数据保留与删除

- 学习进度保存在本设备，直至您 **卸载应用**、**清除应用数据** 或 **恢复设备且不保留应用数据**。
- 卸载应用后，该设备上的本地进度将被删除。
- 我们无法代为恢复已删除的本地数据，因为服务器不保存副本。

### 您的选择

- 在「我的」中关闭音乐或音效。
- 在「我的」中切换界面语言。
- 卸载应用以删除全部本地数据。

### 第三方服务

当前版本未集成第三方登录、广告或分析 SDK。应用通过 **Apple App Store** 分发，适用 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。

若应用内展示可选图包或外部链接（如图标来源说明），点击后将离开本应用，并适用相应网站的政策。

### 境外用户

本应用数据仅保存在您的设备本地，当前版本不会将数据传输至我们的服务器。

### 政策变更

当我们新增功能（例如账号登录或云同步）时，可能更新本政策。我们会更新「最后更新」日期，并在必要时通过应用内或 App Store 页面提示。

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请联系：

**邮箱：** [support@yourdomain.com]
