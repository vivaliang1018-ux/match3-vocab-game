Privacy Policy

Glow & Prompt is provided by the app developer. Effective: 2026-11-19. Contact: vivaliang1018@gmail.com.

This version needs no account and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. We do not upload scripts, audio, video or recognized text to developer servers or use them to train models.

Camera and microphone access is used for recording you initiate. Smart Scroll uses Apple Speech only when on-device recognition is supported; it does not fall back to cloud recognition. Recognized text locates your place in the script and is not kept as a recognition history. Keyboard dictation follows the policies of your selected keyboard and its provider.

Scripts stay on your device and can be deleted on the home screen. Preferences are app-local. Delete saved videos in Photos. Uninstalling normally removes private app data, not videos in Photos. System backups or device migration may include scripts and settings; the current-recording folder is excluded from backup. Keep your own copies of important content.

Apple processes optional tips through in-app purchase. The app verifies Apple transactions; we receive no card details and keep no server-side purchase profile. Apple handles store/payment data under its own privacy policy. Your Apple settings govern system diagnostics and backup.

Revoke permissions in Settings. When you email us, email providers process the message and attachments you choose to send. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information. We use correspondence to respond and resolve your request; contact the same address to request deletion. Policy changes update this date; new data purposes will be disclosed and permission obtained where required.
