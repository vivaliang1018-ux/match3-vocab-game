English

Overview

物品消消乐 is an offline-first vocabulary game. You can play without creating an account. If you sign in, learning progress is stored in Firebase so it can sync across devices. We do not sell your personal information and do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information on our servers. The app stores the following locally on your device only:

DataPurpose
Learning progressSpaced repetition and learned-word stats
Preferences (language, music, SFX)Remember your settings
Onboarding flagShow tutorial only once

This data stays on your device unless included in an Apple device backup (e.g. iCloud).

Information we do not collect

  • Account credentials (no sign-in in v1)
  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
  • Analytics or crash reports to third parties

Speech and audio

The app plays bundled pronunciation audio and may use your device's built-in text-to-speech when a recording is unavailable. Speech is processed on-device; we do not receive microphone audio.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes local progress from that device. We cannot restore deleted local data because we do not host copies.

Questions: support@yourdomain.com

简体中文

概述

「物品消消乐」是一款以本地运行为主的词汇学习游戏。当前版本可不登录使用。 若您选择登录，学习进度会保存到云端以便跨设备同步。我们不会出售您的个人资料，也不使用第三方广告追踪。

我们收集的信息

我们不在服务器上收集个人信息。本应用仅在您的设备本地存储以下信息：

数据用途
学习进度间隔复习与「已学」统计
偏好设置（语言、音乐、音效）记住您的设置
引导完成标记首次安装后只显示一次教程

上述数据默认不会上传至我们的服务器；是否随 iCloud 等设备备份转移取决于 Apple 系统策略。

我们不收集的信息

  • 账号凭证（v1 无登录功能）
  • 地理位置、通讯录、照片或麦克风录音
  • 用于跨应用追踪的广告标识符
  • 发送至第三方分析/崩溃平台的数据

语音与音频

本应用播放内置录音；若无对应录音，将使用设备系统朗读（TTS）。朗读在设备本地处理，我们不会采集麦克风音频。

数据删除

卸载应用后，该设备上的本地进度将被删除。我们无法代为恢复，因为服务器不保存副本。
